- T5.4: Press, sheen, lift, nav underline and the WhatsApp pulse, all CSS in `motion.css`, no new Client Component. `press` is the tap feedback on every `LinkButton`, `ServiceCard`, the menu toggle, footer social icons and the WhatsApp widget (shrinks instantly, eases back over `--motion-fast`, `touch-action: manipulation`). It owns the element's transition, so those elements dropped their own `transition-colors` and `transition-transform`, and other blocks add a duration slot to it. `sheen` is one light sweep on a primary `LinkButton`. `lift` raises a hovered `ServiceCard` 4px and tilts its icon. `underline-draw` draws an accent line under the Navbar links, the Services button (held while its dropdown is open) and the dropdown row titles, on hover and keyboard focus. `pulse-ring` pulses the WhatsApp widget twice (1s each) 2s after a full page load. Every hover rule sits in its own `@media (hover: hover)`, so nothing sticks on touch. `ServiceCard` gained the `LinkButton` keyboard focus ring (the T3.10 follow-up is closed), and the menu toggle and Services button gained `cursor-pointer`. New `@interaction` spec (26 tests) and `InteractionAudit`, and the reduced-motion guard now reads pseudo-elements. 232 Vitest, 109 Playwright per browser (218). See `t5-completion-report.md`
