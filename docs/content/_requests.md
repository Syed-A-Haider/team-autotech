# Content requests for Syd

Tracker for services with no source copy. The old-site pages for these 12 were empty when checked on 2026-11-03. When copy arrives: create `docs/content/<slug>.md` from `_template.md`, open the next content ticket (numbered in order in a later phase, as T3 is closed), and update the row below.

## What we need for each service (minimum to go live)

1. A one-line description (shows on cards and under the page title)
2. Two or three sentences introducing the service: what it is and who it is for
3. One of: 3 to 6 benefits (a short heading and one sentence each), or "how it works" in 3 to 5 steps
4. Your OK on the final wording (we tidy spelling and grammar, and send it back to you)

Nice to have:

- Common customer questions with your answers
- A YouTube video of your own, plus a still image from it (landscape, 16:9)
- A dark landscape photo for the top of the page (no text on it)
- Photos of real installs (if two or more services have them, we build a photo block first)

We never show prices on the site, so please leave them out.

## Questions by service

Remap / Tuning

- DPF / EGR Solutions: exactly what do you offer (diagnosis, cleaning, repair, removal)? Any road-legal or off-road-only conditions we must state? We will only use your wording
- Engine Management Error Code Delete: what does it mean for the customer (clearing codes, or disabling them in the software), and when is it needed?
- Tuning Freaks Bootmod: which platform, which BMW models or engines, which stages?

Programming (BMW, Mercedes, VAG)

- What is included: keys, coding, retrofits, module replacement? Which models and years? For VAG, which brands?

Auto Security

- Spectre Immobiliser: how it works, how the driver disarms it, which vehicles
- Scorpion Trackers: which models? What does "insurance approved" mean (which approval)? Is a subscription needed (no price shown)?

Auto Electrical (car audio, dashcams, parking sensors, reverse cameras)

- Supply and fit, or fit only? Which brands? Front, rear or both? Hardwired? Typical fitting time?
