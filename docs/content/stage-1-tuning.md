## Prose: What is Stage 1 Tuning?

Stage 1 tuning refers to performance modifications that can be made to your car's engine and ECU (Engine Control Unit) to increase horsepower, torque, and fuel efficiency. Unlike more extensive tuning options (Stage 2 or Stage 3), Stage 1 generally involves software modifications like remapping or chip tuning and sometimes light upgrades to air intake or exhaust systems. This makes it an ideal choice for those looking to gain more performance without going overboard.

## Features (list): Key Benefits of Stage 1 Tuning

- Increased Power: The most significant advantage of Stage 1 tuning is the noticeable increase in horsepower and torque. Expect a boost of 10-20% depending on your vehicle.
- Improved Throttle Response: Stage 1 tuning can sharpen throttle response, giving you a more immediate and engaging driving experience.
- Better Fuel Efficiency: While Stage 1 tuning is typically aimed at improving performance, the ECU remap can also optimize fuel usage, leading to better miles per gallon (MPG) in some cases.
- Quick and Easy: Stage 1 tuning is often done through a simple ECU remap, which means no invasive mechanical work is required. The tuning can be completed in a short amount of time, often taking only an hour or two.
