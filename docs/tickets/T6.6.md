## The standard (goes into `_standard.md`)

- **Voice:** British English, second person, energetic but natural, short sentences, no hype. "Nelson, Lancashire" at most once, naturally. No em dashes. Avoid "unleash", "elevate", "seamless", "look no further", "best in the UK", "cheapest"
- **Structure per page:** `shortDescription` (one sentence, about 70 to 150 characters, no claims, doubles as meta description). A `prose` intro (2 to 3 paragraphs). One `feature` block (`grid` 3 to 6 items, `alternating` 2 to 4, `list` 4 to 8). Optional `steps` (3 to 5) for process-led services. `faq` with 3 to 5 questions, the pricing answer always "pricing depends on your vehicle and spec, call or WhatsApp for a free, no-obligation quote". `specband` only with facts from the constants. No `video` unless Syd supplies one. Vary the mix and order between pages. Feature grids stay at 6 items or fewer (the layout plan only avoids a lone last-row card for 1 to 6)
- **Generic copy is allowed:** what the service is and why people want it, the typical process, what to bring, enquiry via Call or WhatsApp, scope phrased as "can include ... depending on your vehicle, ask us about yours"
- **Never invented (needs Syd):** prices, ranges or discounts. Turnaround or fitting times. Power, torque, MPG or percentage gains. Accreditations, approvals or memberships (Thatcham, "insurance approved", "authorised" or "official" beyond titles Syd gave). Warranties or guarantees. Vehicle coverage lists, product specs, brands of tools or parts. Staff names or numbers, years other than `YEARS_EXPERIENCE`, customer counts, testimonials. Mobile or on-site service. Insurance effects. Legal or compliance claims ("road legal", "MOT pass", "emissions compliant"), and anything about removing or deleting emissions equipment. Any claim to be an MOT testing station
- Brand names (Ghost, Scorpion, Road Angel, CT Carbon, Tuning Freaks, XHP) only name the product

## Copy guard

`services.content.test.ts` walks every string in `shortDescription` and `sections` and fails on: `£`, a number followed by hp, bhp, Nm, mpg or %, guarantee, warranty, Thatcham, approved, accredited, certified, official or authorised (with an allow-list for titles Syd gave), same day, cheapest, delete or remov- near DPF, EGR or AdBlue, "MOT pass". It also checks `shortDescription` length and a short list of US spellings. Run it red against today's data first: whatever it flags goes to Syd.

## Review tracking

`_review.md`: one row per page (the three live pages, About and the homepage claims included): page, ticket or PR, source (old site or AI), facts to confirm, status (awaiting, changes requested, approved, with date). Each AI entry in `services.ts` carries a one-line `// AI-generated (T6.x), awaiting Syd's review` comment. Visitors never see either. `status.md` "Waiting on Syd" points at the table.

## Out of scope

Writing any page copy. Changing the blocks.
