## The standard (goes into `_standard.md`)

- **Voice:** British English, second person, energetic but natural, short sentences, no hype. "Nelson, Lancashire" at most once per page. No em dashes. Banned: "unleash", "elevate", "seamless", "look no further", "best in the UK", "cheapest"
- **Structure per page:** `shortDescription` (one sentence, 70 to 150 characters, no claims, doubles as the meta description). A `prose` intro (2 to 3 paragraphs). One `feature` block (`grid` 3 to 6 items, `alternating` 2 to 4, `list` 4 to 8). Optional `steps` (3 to 5). `faq` with 3 to 5 questions, the pricing answer always "Pricing depends on your vehicle and spec. Call or WhatsApp for a free, no-obligation quote." `specband` only with facts from the constants. No `video` unless Syd supplies one. Vary the mix and order between pages. Grids stay at 6 items or fewer
- **Generic copy is allowed:** what the service is, the typical process, what to bring, enquiry via Call or WhatsApp, scope phrased as "can include ... depending on your vehicle, ask us about yours"
- **Never invented:** prices, turnaround or fitting times, power, torque, MPG or percentage gains, accreditations, warranties or guarantees, vehicle lists, product specs, tool or part brands, staff names, years other than `YEARS_EXPERIENCE`, customer counts, mobile or on-site service, insurance effects, legal or compliance claims, anything about removing or disabling emissions equipment, any claim to be an MOT testing station
- **Confirmed claims (Haider, 2026-11-07),** allowed only where they already appear: "Authorised Ghost Immobiliser II Installer", "backed by our guarantee", "family-run", "genuine manufacturer-grade tools", "Insurance-approved GPS tracking systems"
- Brand names only name the product

## Copy guard (`services.content.test.ts`)

- Reads each service from `getAllServices()` and walks `shortDescription` and `sections`. The walker skips the keys `type`, `layout`, `videoId`, `posterImage` and `icon` and checks every other string, so a new copy field is covered by itself
- A flat `COPY_RULES` list. A failure prints the slug, the field path, the rule and the match. Patterns are case-insensitive, never carry the `g` flag, and a leading word boundary lets "unapproved" and "unauthorised" pass
- Rules: `price` (`£`, GBP), `figure` (a number then %, per cent, hp, bhp, PS, kW, Nm, lb-ft, mpg, mph), `accreditation` (approv, accredit, certified, certification, official, authoris, Thatcham), `guarantee` (guarant, warrant), `turnaround` (same day, a number then hour, minute, day or week, "an hour", "while you wait"), `emissions-removal` (DPF, EGR or AdBlue within 6 words of remov, delet, disabl, bypass or blank, or "off" straight after), `legal` (MOT pass, road legal, emissions compliant), `voice` (the banned words and the em dash), `superlative` (market-leading, industry-leading, most advanced, UK's leading, number one, unrivalled, unbeatable, world-class), `us-spelling` (a short specific list)
- Two page-level rules: `place` ("Nelson, Lancashire" more than once on a page) and `short-description-length` (70 to 150 characters, live pages only, because the coming-soon one-liners restate the title until their batch rewrites them)
- Allow-list `ALLOWED_COPY`: slug + rule + exact phrase, `confirmed` or `awaiting-syd`, with a note. The phrase is blanked out for that page and that rule only, so a confirmed claim cannot spread to another page or excuse a second risk in the same string. A length waiver holds only while the description is exactly that phrase. A stale check fails when an entry's slug is gone or its phrase no longer appears. New entries need Haider's OK and a `_review.md` row
- Red first: with an empty allow-list the sweep failed on exactly 11 hits across five pages (Stage 1 Tuning 7, Ghost 1, Scorpion X 1, Scorpion Trackers 1, CT Carbon 1). Syd's answer was to let them stand, so each became an `awaiting-syd` entry (or `confirmed` for the two titles and claims) and a row in `_review.md`
- Not guarded: structure counts (FAQ, grid, paragraphs), component copy (`WhyChooseUsSection`, `AboutSnippet`, About, `siteConfig.description`), `reviews.ts`, the `// AI-generated` comment. They stay in the standard and `_review.md`

## Review tracking (`_review.md`)

One row per page: the three live pages, About, the homepage claims and the 21 coming-soon pages. Columns: page, path, ticket or PR, source (old site or AI), facts to check, status (`not written`, `awaiting`, `changes requested`, `approved YYYY-MM-DD`). Awaiting does not block anything: pages stay live. Each AI entry in `services.ts` carries `// AI-generated (T6.x), awaiting Syd's review`. Visitors never see either. `status.md` "Waiting on Syd" points at the table.

## Tests

Walker (paths and skipped keys on a fixture with all six block types), a failing and a passing string per rule, upper case, the `place` and length rules (69, 70, 150, 151, coming-soon), the allow-list (own page, another page, own rule only, length waiver, stale check) and one real-data case per slug.

## Out of scope

Writing any page copy, changing blocks or types, guarding component copy, structure-count checks, enforcing the AI comment.
