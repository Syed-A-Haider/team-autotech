## Blocks (each inside the scroll guard: no-preference + `@supports (animation-timeline: view())`)

- `reveal`: fade and rise by `--reveal-y`. `animation: reveal-up linear backwards`, then `animation-timeline: view()`, then `animation-range: entry 0% entry 70%`
- `reveal-left` and `reveal-right`: same, sliding by `--reveal-x`. Use the individual `translate` property so it composes with `-skew-*`
- `reveal-stagger` (on a parent): sets `--i` 0 to 5 on `:nth-child` (literal list, no inline styles) and shifts each child's range by `calc(var(--i) * var(--stagger))`. Children get `reveal`. `animation-delay` does nothing on scroll timelines
- `stripe-draw`: `scale` 0 to 1 on X from the left, for the skewed stripe

## Spike (prove each on a production build; record results in the report)

1. A reveal inside `overflow-clip` animates, and inside `overflow-hidden` it does not (an `overflow-hidden` ancestor becomes the timeline's scroller). Then switch `SectionShell` and `EnquiryBand` to `overflow-clip` and check nothing else changed
2. `animation` shorthand then `animation-timeline` order holds inside one `@utility`
3. A `scale` stripe composes with `-skew-x-12`
4. iOS Safari 26 and Chrome Android: address-bar resize does not stall the timeline. Never put `view()` on a `sticky` or `fixed` subject
5. `position: fixed` stays correct after the range ends (no held transform)
6. The last block above the footer reaches the end of its range
7. Scrolling back up: the reverse at the bottom edge looks acceptable on a phone. If not, shorten the range
8. Add `scroll-padding-top: 4rem` to `html` so `/#services` clears the sticky Navbar
9. No sideways scroll from side slides at 375
